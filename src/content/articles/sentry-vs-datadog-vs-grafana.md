This is a test draft to verify the admin dashboard. Sentry excels at error tracking. Datadog is a full observability platform. Grafana is the open-source visualization layer. We'll compare pricing, setup complexity, and which use case each serves best.

## Quick Comparison

Sentry starts at $26/mo for teams and focuses specifically on error tracking and performance. Datadog is priced per host ($15/host/mo for Pro) and covers the full observability stack. Grafana Cloud has a generous free tier with Grafana itself being open-source.

## When to Choose Each

**Sentry** — Your primary need is error tracking and frontend monitoring. Best DX for debugging.

**Datadog** — You need the full stack: logs, metrics, traces, APM. Budget is less of a concern.

**Grafana** — You want flexibility, self-hosting option, or already use the Prometheus/PromQL ecosystem.
